Deep Ocean
(Animation)

An educational animation for Aves Park in Štiavnické Bane showcasing the beauty of the underwater world and the impact of plastic waste on marine life.

Fresh Water Life (Interactive Animation)

An interactive animation for Aves Park in Štiavnické Bane where children color a black-and-white paper drawing of a freshwater fish, scan it, and watch their exact creation appear and swim in an animated underwater scene projected on the exhibition wall.

The project included creating the freshwater underwater world and implementing interactive controls.
